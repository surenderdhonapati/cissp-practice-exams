The adaptive engine imitates the behaviour of a computerised adaptive test and does not reproduce ISC2's scoring. A practice verdict does not predict your result in the real exam.

  • Adaptive: the next item depends on how you answered so far. Four options, one answer, no going back.
  • Between 100 and 150 items. The exam stops with a pass or a fail verdict as soon as your ability estimate is clearly above or below the pass standard, or at item 150.
  • 180 minutes on the clock, no feedback during the exam. If the time runs out before item 100 the attempt is a fail, as in the real exam; after item 100 the verdict is decided by your ability estimate at that moment.
  • Three pauses per attempt, as long as you like. Closing the tab, or leaving the page hidden for more than thirty seconds, counts as a pause and costs five seconds. When all three pauses are used and you leave again, the attempt ends.
  • After the verdict you may continue with the remaining items without time pressure; they are scored separately and never change the verdict.
  • Everything stays in this browser on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere. The saved progress is lost in a private window or when site data is cleared.
